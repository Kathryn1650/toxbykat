Process

What actually happens during a consult

By Kat, RN

A common question I get: how much of this is decided on the day of the consult versus the day of treatment? Here's an honest walkthrough of both — and if you've already been through a consult with me before, scroll to the bottom for how to skip straight to booking your next visit.

The consult

A free, no-pressure conversation — no needles involved.

  • We talk first

    Before anything else, I ask what's actually bothering you — and just as importantly, what isn't. Some people want everything softened; others just want one specific line addressed. There's no assumption either way.

  • You get a rough estimate

    Based on that conversation, I'll give you a general, "probable" sense of what treatment might look like — but nothing is finalized here. The exact plan gets built at your treatment visit, once I can actually see how your muscles move that day.

The treatment visit

This is where the real mapping and the injections happen.

  • I map your movement

    I'll ask you to raise your brows, frown, and smile naturally while I watch how your muscles actually move that day. This is where your exact plan gets built — not from the earlier estimate alone, but from what I see in front of me.

  • We confirm the plan before I pick up a needle

    I'll walk you through the exact units I'm recommending and why, so there are no surprises. If you want to go more conservative, we adjust — it's your face and your call.

  • The treatment itself

    Quick — most appointments take 15 to 30 minutes total, and the injections themselves are done in just a few minutes. Everything is single-use and sterile, prepped fresh at your location.

  • Aftercare

    I'll send you home with simple aftercare notes — avoid touching the treated area for a few hours, skip intense exercise for the rest of the day, and stay upright for the first four hours. Results typically show up within three to five days, fully settled by two weeks.
